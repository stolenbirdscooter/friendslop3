# Devlog

## v01 — First Flight
Design pillars chosen for a friendslop game that is *not* a horror-scavenger,
not a climbing game, not a ragdoll brawler:
1. **One shared, under-steerable vehicle.** Altitude is the steering wheel (real
   balloon physics: wind layers). Forces conversation: "go UP, the 60 m wind
   blows toward Gullhaven!"
2. **Your body is a game piece.** Crew weight tips the basket (pendulum about the
   envelope ring) — crowding a side sends parcels and people sliding.
3. **Failure is funny, not punishing.** Falling = puff up and glide, or splash
   into the fluff and get reinflated. Throws that miss get *spat back* by the
   chute ("RETURN TO SENDER").
4. **Expressiveness without voice chat.** Pitch-controlled rubber squeak, shoves.
5. **Storybook-handmade look.** Toon bands with cool ambient shadows, ink
   outlines, patchwork envelope shader, postage-stamp UI.
